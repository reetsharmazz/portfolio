# 🏰 Reet Sharma — Chronicles of the Realm Portfolio

A medieval-fantasy portfolio website designed in **HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript**, crafted around the **Game of Thrones universe** with soft, subtle tones, authentic lore, and interactive interfaces.

---

## 🗺️ The Three Interfaces & GoT Strongholds

1. **🏰 Winterfell (The North) — *My Details***
   - **Theme**: Resilience, authenticity, and steadfast foundations.
   - **Features**: Maester's Ledger, Date of Birth (*26 / 09 / 2006*), House Virtues, direct Electronic Raven address, and one-click copy scroll action.
2. **📜 The Citadel of Oldtown (The Reach) — *About Me***
   - **Theme**: Knowledge, intellectual curiosity, and forging links from scratch.
   - **Features**: Rich narrative highlighting Reet's identity as a student starting from zero coding background, a passionate traveler, and a master of organization and management. Includes an interactive breakdown of the *Maester's Chain of Skills*.
3. **🌊 Braavos (Across the Narrow Sea) — *My Profiles & Expeditions***
   - **Theme**: Exploration, cosmopolitan connections, and trade winds.
   - **Features**: Harbor cards for professional profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram/Travel Diary, Email), plus *The Traveler's Compass*.

---

## 🎨 Design & Atmosphere Highlights
- **Subtle, Soft Color Palette**: Muted slate greys, soft frosty mist, antique warm parchment, gentle bronze gold, and sea salt teals.
- **Dynamic Particle Atmosphere**:
  - Gentle falling snow over *Winterfell*.
  - Floating warm library dust motes over *The Citadel*.
  - Gentle sea mist breeze over *Braavos*.
- **Procedural Ambient Lute (Web Audio API)**:
  - Toggle soft medieval harp/lute chords with 0 external sound files.
- **Electronic Raven Modal**:
  - An interactive parchment form to compose and dispatch messages directly to your email inbox.
- **Keyboard Navigation**:
  - Use `ArrowLeft` and `ArrowRight` to travel between realms.
  - Press `Esc` to dismiss modals.

## 🚀 How to View Locally
Simply double-click [`index.html`](index.html) or open it in any web browser:
```bash
open index.html
```

Or run a local server:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
